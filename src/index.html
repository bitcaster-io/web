Introducing Bitcaster
The multichannel broker for managing corporate messaging

Bitcaster is a system-to-user signal-to-message notification system which comes loaded with multiple ready to use dispatchers and channel plugins. Communicate safely and efficiently with your users without the problem of choosing the right channel.

How it works

1

Bitcaster receives signals from any of your applications/systems using a simple RESTful API

2

It converts them in messages to be distributed to your users via a plethora of channels

3

The system customizes messages at user/receiver level using a flexible template system

4

And finally empowers the user with an easy to use console to choose how to receive the messages configured in Bitcaster

With Bitcaster you can

Avoid to keep the contact details of the recipients up to date

Be relieved of responsibility for data retention and security

Simplify your applications by decoupling them from the various transport systems

Decrease operating costs and increase productivity
